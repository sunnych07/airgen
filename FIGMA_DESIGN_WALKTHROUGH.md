## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Process: How to Put This Into Figma (Under 2 Minutes)

### Method 1: The Instant Drag-and-Drop Method (Recommended & Easiest)
1. Open **Figma** (desktop app or in your browser at [figma.com](https://figma.com)).
2. Create a **New Design File** (`Untitled`).
3. Open Windows File Explorer and navigate to:
   `C:\airgen\figma_exports`
4. Select all 6 SVG files:
   - `screen1_hero.svg`
   - `screen2_flightpath.svg`
   - `screen3_eligibility_calculator.svg`
   - `screen4_training_bases.svg`
   - `screen5_alumni_wings.svg`
   - `screen6_admissions_hub.svg`
5. **Drag and drop them directly onto your Figma canvas!**
6. Figma will instantly convert them into 6 native 1440x900px Frames with all shapes, gradients, icons, and text layers preserved and fully editable.
7. Arrange the 6 frames horizontally in order from Screen 1 to Screen 6 with a 100px gap between them.

## 🎨 Design System & Visual Strategy

### Why this design beats the old site (`https://version-1-s4z5.onrender.com/`):
- **The Old Site's Mistake**: Pitch-black backgrounds with bloody crimson red accents (`#bf1e2e`), cryptic radar circles, and heavy dark gaming vibes. For 17-18 year-old 12th passouts and their parents (who are investing ₹45L–₹60L), this felt dark, intimidating, and untrustworthy.
- **The Redesign Solution**:
  - **Aero Navy (`#071224`, `#0D2245`)**: Evokes the prestige of the flight deck, night sky, and institutional security.
  - **Stratosphere Blue & Sky Cyan (`#0284C7`, `#38BDF8`)**: High altitude clarity, modern aviation technology.
  - **4-Bar Flight Gold (`#FBBF24`)**: Captain epaulets, achievement, and excellence.
  - **Avionics Emerald (`#10B981`)**: Regulatory compliance, DGCA clearance, verified pass rates.

### Mandatory Brand Requirement:
> **"Airgen brand name must be present on every slide/screen of your design."**
- Every single screen features the **AirGen Aviation** logo in the top navigation bar, the contextual header, the bottom footer, and the official bottom watermark.

## 📱 The 6 Core Screens Breakdown (What to Say to the Judges)

### Screen 1: Hero & Strategic Value Anchor
- **Key Hook**: *"From 12th Grade to the Airline Flight Deck."*
- **The Problem Solved**: Immediately answers who AirGen is for (post-12th students) and what they deliver (commercial airline pilot careers).
- **Core Elements**:
  - Top Navigation with AirGen logo and "DGCA Cadet Batch 2026 Admissions Open".
  - **80+ Graduated Pilots Trust Badge** right in the hero.
  - Dual High-Conversion CTAs: *"Check 12th Eligibility"* and *"Download Cadet Prospectus"*.
  - Cockpit Artificial Horizon & Speed/Alt HUD instrument widget.
  - High-Prestige Metric Strip: 80+ Airline Pilots, 100% DGCA Pass Rate, 18-22 Months Timeline, 9 Global Bases.

### Screen 2: 5-Stage Pilot Career Flightpath
- **The Problem Solved**: High school students and parents are overwhelmed by aviation jargon. They want to know the step-by-step chronological roadmap.
- **The 5 Stages**:
  1. *Stage 01: Eligibility & DGCA Medicals* (Month 1-2)
  2. *Stage 02: Precision Ground School & DGCA Exams* (Month 3-6)
  3. *Stage 03: 200+ Flying Hours in USA / South Africa* (Month 7-14)
  4. *Stage 04: Multi-Engine Rating & DGCA CPL Conversion* (Month 15-17)
  5. *Stage 05: Type Rating & First Officer Seat* (Month 18-22)

### Screen 3: Interactive 12th-Grade Eligibility & ROI Calculator
- **The Problem Solved**: 90% of students drop off because they don't know if their 12th marks or non-PCM background qualify them, or whether glasses are allowed.
- **The Interactive Solution**:
  - Students select their stream (PCM vs Non-PCM/NIOS), Age, and Medical fitness.
  - Live "Cleared for Pilot Training" dashboard shows custom timelines, exam exemptions, and full Day-1 cost transparency with zero hidden fees.

### Screen 4: Global Flight Training Fleet & International Bases
- **The Problem Solved**: Parents worry about foreign flight schools and weather delays.
- **The Solution**:
  - **Phoenix, Arizona Base (USA)**: 350 sunny flying days/year, Cessna 172SP Garmin G1000 glass cockpit, 55+ AirGen cadets trained.
  - **Johannesburg Base (South Africa)**: Cost-effective high ROI base, 25+ cadets trained.
  - **India HQ (Hyderabad/Delhi)**: ALSIM ALX flight simulators, precision ground school, DGCA conversion wing.

### Screen 5: The Hall of Wings (80+ Alumni Placements)
- **The Problem Solved**: Proof of outcomes is the single biggest conversion factor for parents.
- **The Solution**:
  - Real cadet profiles: Capt. Aarav Singhania (IndiGo A320), FO Sneha Deshmukh (Air India B787), FO Rohan Varma (Akasa Air B737).
  - Verified alumni IDs, graduation timeline, and inspiring video quotes.
  - Commercial airline logos bar: IndiGo, Air India, Akasa Air, SpiceJet, Air India Express.

### Screen 6: Admissions Hub, Parental Guidance & FAQs
- **The Problem Solved**: Securing parental trust and financing clarity.
- **The Solution**:
  - Direct 1-on-1 Consultation Booking with active Airline Captains.
  - Parents' FAQ Accordion addressing safety abroad, education loan partnerships with SBI & HDFC Credila, and DGCA regulatory compliance.
  - Official accreditations seals (DGCA, FAA, SACAA, ICAO) and corporate footer.
